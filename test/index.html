Control lab — interactive

Flip the switches and press Restart: every service the card would send to Home Assistant is logged on the right. The counter to watch is card rebuilds — Home Assistant calls set hass on every state change in your install, so press Fire unrelated update repeatedly: ticks should climb while rebuilds stay put. Slow service calls holds the call open so you can see the pending row: the switch stays in the position you asked for and the controls stay disabled until Docker answers.
